# Wellmaxx support

Wellmaxx is an iPhone health dashboard. It turns your Apple Health data into daily Readiness, Sleep and Effort scores, with a food log and a workout log, and keeps it all on your iPhone.

This is where to get help with the app, and where its privacy policy is published.

- **[Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)**
- **[Ask a question or report a problem](https://github.com/wikoion/wellmaxx-support/issues/new)** (needs a free GitHub account)

## Getting help

Open an issue with the link above. It helps to say:

- your iPhone model and iOS version;
- which watch or band writes your data to Apple Health;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened.

Issues are public. Please leave out health data, API keys and screenshots that show anything you'd rather keep private.

## Common questions

**My scores are empty.** Wellmaxx works out its scores from Apple Health, so it needs a watch or band that writes sleep and heart data there, and your permission to read it. If a number stays empty, check that type's permission in the Health app; More → Devices shows what each device recorded. Readiness also needs a few nights of data before its first score. The Food Log and the Workout tab work without any Apple Health data.

**Where is my data kept?** In the app's database on your iPhone. There is no Wellmaxx account and no Wellmaxx server. The [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) lists each feature that can send data elsewhere, all of them off until you turn them on or add your own key, apart from food search through Open Food Facts, which you can turn off in Food Log settings.

**How do I move to a new iPhone?** On the old phone, go to More → Backup → Export everything (ZIP) and save the file somewhere the new phone can reach. On the new phone, use More → Backup → Restore from export.

**How do I delete my data?** More → Backup → Delete all data removes everything the app stored on your iPhone (Apple Health itself isn't touched). Deleting the app does the same. Copies you sent elsewhere are yours to delete there.
